Common questions

How do I ride with my friends?

One rider creates a pack on the Rides tab and shares its code or invite link. Everyone else taps "Join pack with code" or opens the link. When someone starts a ride, the rest of the pack gets a notification and can join the live map.

Who can see where I am?

Only riders in the same live ride as you. Your position is shared only while you're in a ride and stops the moment you leave it or the ride ends. We do not keep that live trail. After the ride, your phone saves a simplified path in your logbook. Only you can see that path and your top speed. Riders who were on the ride with you can see your distance, moving time and lean in the pack recap. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why does MotoGrid ask for "Always" location access?

So the pack keeps seeing you while your screen is locked or you're in another app during a ride. MotoGrid uses location in the background only during a ride, and iOS shows the location indicator while it does.

How much battery does it use?

A ride uses GPS the whole time, like any navigation app. On long rides keep your phone on a charger. Your position goes out every few seconds while you're moving and only now and then while you're stopped, which keeps mobile data low.

I lost signal. What happens?

MotoGrid reconnects on its own once you're back in coverage. While a rider has no signal, their marker turns grey; it comes back as soon as their phone reconnects.

How do I move my account to a new phone?

Open Garage → Profile and tap "Continue with Apple" to keep your account with your Apple ID. On the new phone, choose "Sign in with Apple" on the first screen, and your profile, garage, friends and packs come back.

How do I report or block someone?

Open the rider's card and tap Report or Block rider. A pack can be reported from its menu on the Rides tab. We review reports within 24 hours and remove riders who break the Terms.

My account is suspended. What can I do?

Accounts are suspended after reports from other riders. If you think it's a mistake, write to motogrid@kvasov.us with your rider tag, and we'll take another look.

How do I delete my account?

Open Garage → Profile and tap "Delete account" at the bottom. Your profile, garage, photos, friends, messages and ride logbook are deleted for good. Packs you lead pass to the next rider.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

MotoGrid: Group Ride Radar ("MotoGrid", "we", "us") lets motorcycle riders share their live position with the group they're riding with. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it and how long we keep it.

The short version. Your live location is shared only with riders in the same ride, only during that ride, and is never stored on our servers. We don't show ads, don't track you across apps or websites, don't use analytics, and never sell your data.

What we collect

Your account

MotoGrid creates an account without asking for your email or phone number. If you choose to keep it with Sign in with Apple, we receive an identifier from Apple and the email address Apple shares with us, which may be a private relay address. If Apple shares your name, we only use it to fill in your rider name, and you can change it.

Your profile and garage

Your rider name, rider tag, profile photo and, if you add them, your city, Instagram handle and speed unit. Your motorcycles: year, make, model, style, nickname, notes such as engine, exhaust, tires and mods, and photos.

Packs, friends and rides

The packs you belong to, your friends and the riders you block, ride invites, the rides you take part in, the meeting point, stops and destination of a ride (place names, addresses and coordinates), short messages sent in a ride, and arrival notices such as "arrived at the destination".

Live location

During a ride, your phone sends your position, speed and heading to the other riders in that ride every few seconds. It passes through our real-time service and is not written to our database. MotoGrid uses location in the background only while you're in a ride, so the pack keeps seeing you when your screen is locked. Outside a ride, your location is used only on your phone, for example to show you on the map, search for places near you and plan a route.

Reports

If you report a rider or a pack, we keep the report, the reason and any details you write, so we can review it.

Device data

A push notification token, so we can send you ride and friend notifications. A DeviceCheck token from Apple, which we use to keep riders banned for abuse from coming back on the same phone. DeviceCheck lets us store two yes/no values per device with Apple; it does not identify you or the device to us.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show riders on the map, run packs, rides and routes, deliver messages and notifications.
  • To keep riders safe: review reports, remove content and ban riders who break the Terms of Use.
  • To answer you when you write to support.

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or training models, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Who can see what

  • Live location: only riders who are in the same live ride as you.
  • Any rider who meets you in the app (for example in a pack or a ride) can see your rider name, rider tag, profile photo and the make and model of your main bike.
  • Your friends can also see your city, Instagram handle, full bike details and photos, and whether you're riding right now.
  • Ride stops, destination and messages are visible to the riders in that ride.
  • Photos are stored at addresses that are hard to guess but not secret, so anyone who has the exact link can open them.
  • Riders you block don't find you in search, can't send you friend requests and never end up in the same ride as you.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase hosts our database, accounts, photo storage and the real-time service that carries live positions.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, push notifications, DeviceCheck, and Apple Maps. Place search, addresses and route directions are requested from Apple Maps, which receives your search text and location for that purpose under Apple's Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of riders.

How long we keep it

  • Live positions are not stored.
  • A ride, with its route, stops, messages and invites, is deleted 30 days after it ends.
  • Notification records are deleted after 7 days.
  • A pack that everyone has left is deleted 30 days later, unless a report about it is still open.
  • An account that never finished setting up is deleted after 7 days.
  • Everything else stays until you delete it or your account.

Deleting your account

Open Garage → Profile and tap "Delete account". This deletes your account, profile, photos, garage, friends and blocks, pack memberships, messages, invites and device tokens, and the rides you started once they're over. Packs you lead pass to the next rider. If your account is kept with your Apple ID, MotoGrid is also signed out of it with Apple.

Reports you made about others are kept for moderation without your name. If an account was banned for good for abuse, the DeviceCheck value on that phone stays with Apple so the ban holds.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and garage any time in the app.
  • Turn off location or notifications in iOS Settings. Without location you can't share your position on a ride.
  • Leave a ride or a pack, or block a rider, at any time.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it by writing to motogrid@kvasov.us. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, and we honor them for everyone.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access is limited by database rules, so riders only reach what's described above. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as required by law if something goes wrong.

Children

MotoGrid is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, let you know in the app.

Contact
